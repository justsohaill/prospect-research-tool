Questions? Answered.

01

What do you actually do?

We run your whole LinkedIn acquisition system: content, DMs, lead magnets and inbound. One team, one goal: booked calls with qualified buyers.

02

Who do you work with?

AI and B2B founders offering high-ticket services, selling high-ticket offers, with an offer that already sells.

03

How is this different from a content agency?

Content agencies stop at posts. We start conversations with your exact buyers every day and book the calls. Content just makes those DMs land.

04

How does pricing work?

One flat monthly fee, everything included, over a 90-day launch. No percentage deals. We'll walk you through it on the call.

05

Why 90 days?

Month one is for testing and building. Month two scales what works. By month three, content and DMs feed each other into a steady flow of calls. Judging LinkedIn on its first month is like judging a rocket on the launchpad.

06

How much of my time does it take?

One onboarding call to start. After that, content and DMs go live from day 2, and you show up for the calls.

07

What if you don't hit 15 qualified calls?

You choose. If the needle has moved, month 2 is free. If you're not happy with the quality, we refund 60% of what you paid and part ways. "Qualified" is agreed in writing on day 1, so there's no arguing later.
