# Style DNA: the reference graphics, taken apart

These seven graphics set the quality bar. They are described here so you understand **why
they work**. They are not templates. Never rebuild one of them with new words.

---

## R1. "Deloitte-Style AI Marketing Team." (org chart, STUDIO theme)
- **Hook:** borrowed authority ("Deloitte-Style") + subject. A heavy black display font with
  very tight tracking. "Team" gets a green gradient, and both lines end in a **green full stop**.
- **Sub:** "12 departments. 60 AI agents. All inside ✻ Claude Code." Numbers first, product
  mark inline.
- **Hero:** a dark "CMO" command pill (orb icon, `CMO` tag, title, "LIVE" status at
  right), then org-chart wires, then **12 department columns × 5 agents = 60 items**. Each
  department has its own saturated header colour (green, blue, indigo, violet, red, orange,
  yellow, coral, teal, sky, brown, grey) with an icon and a check. Each agent is a white
  row with a coloured check, a **mono kebab-case name** (`pricing-watcher`) and a grey
  4-word description.
- **Motion:** the status reads "ROUTING TO 15/60 Competitor Intel" and counts up while a
  highlight moves through the departments, then ends on "All 60 agents done".
- **CTA:** a black pill with a rainbow conic glow: ✻ Comment "MARKETING" (lime) for the
  full team, plus a white arrow circle. Small grey line underneath: "Claude Code plans it ·
  60 agents build it · You approve it".
- **Lessons:** a grid of 60 items still reads clean because the colour system does the
  grouping. Mono names make it feel real and technical.

## R2. "Every B2B Company Needs a GTM Engineer" (train station, dark custom theme)
- **Hook:** a universal claim. Regular weight with the key noun in **bold**. Sub: "The
  Whole System to Steal, Running on One Timetable".
- **Metaphor (fully mapped):** a DEPARTURES split-flap board = cron jobs (`00:30 TAM`,
  `02:20 ENRICHMENT` …, all "Daily"). ARRIVALS = metrics to watch, each with a progress bar.
  **Train cars** = tool categories (Data, Signals, Enrichment, Research, Sequencing,
  Build), each carrying 3 logo tiles, pulled by a "GTM ENGINEER" locomotive. A **playbook
  rail line** with station stops (CRM → ICP model → … → Closed won). **Tracks splitting**
  into TIER 1/2/3 lead to a "TERMINUS: Closed Won" sign. Bottom: 4 explainer cards, a
  signal light, a station clock, the author standing on the platform, and a walking
  pictogram.
- **Motion:** split-flap letters scramble into place, trains move along the tiers, the
  signal light changes, the walker crosses.
- **Lessons:** the metaphor is used **completely**: every part of a station carries
  content. Industrial details (truss roof, rails, sleepers, star field) add richness
  without noise.

## R3. "The LinkedIn Content Machine" (network map, PAPER-pink editorial)
- **Hook:** "The LinkedIn Content **Machine**". Light/regular display at a very large size,
  very tight tracking, with the last word on a **hot-pink highlight block**. Brand mark top left.
- **Hero:** a free-form **network graph** of the system: inputs (Sales calls 179, My posts
  735, rendered as dotted spheres), "The brain" (lime node), skills in mono
  `{hook-generator}`, tools (apify, exa, gemini, claude code), outputs (Outbound: clay,
  prospeo, fullenrich…). Thin wires connect everything. Small grey counts after every label.
- **Stats:** two lime boxes with thin black borders. "Team followers 161.0K" and "Meetings
  from content 34+ a month", with **counters animating** up from 0.
- **Footer:** author headshot, name and domain, plus a right-aligned spaced-out mono caps
  proof line: "GROWN FROM THE SYSTEM BEHIND 24 PEOPLE POSTING AND $153K MRR IN 87 DAYS".
- **Lessons:** a pink-to-cream wash, crop marks and lots of whitespace give a premium
  editorial feel. Mono and sans together make it read as a "system".

## R4. "Who leads sales at the Forbes AI 50." (radial roster, PAPER grid)
- **Hook:** a question about a famous list, regular weight, ending in a full stop.
- **Legend:** mono caps with coloured squares: SECURITY AND DATA (8) · ENTERPRISE
  PLATFORMS (3) · AI COMPANIES (3) · EVERYTHING ELSE (10).
- **Hero:** a ring of ~24 **headshots** around a centre badge ("F / AI 50 / 2026"). Each
  photo has a small company badge, a name and a tiny mono company label. Company logo tiles
  sit outside the ring, linked by curved dotted connectors. **Category arcs** (red, black,
  grey) with end caps wrap the ring like a dimension line.
- **Motion:** the ring slowly rotates and the arcs draw in.
- **Lessons:** a roster becomes a diagram once it gets geometry plus a category system.
  Faces drive the stop-scroll.

## R5. "2026 ABM + Ads Playbook" (glass funnel machine, INK)
- **Hook:** year + topic + "Playbook". A light serif-like display, with the first words
  in the accent blue.
- **Hero:** a 3D **glass funnel** filled with grey cubes. Cubes turn blue as they pass
  filter stages (Broad TAM → 1st party → 2nd party → 3rd party). Neon blue **pipes**
  carry them to glass **tanks** (Tier 1·1:1, Tier 2·1:few, Tier 3·1:many) holding channel
  logos. Step boxes on the left: `STEP 01 · TAM SCOPING` (mono caps) above a serif step
  title and bullets with logo tiles. The "most engaged" tank shows a mock Slack
  notification ("Meeting booked +$72,000 pipeline"). The final tank: "Close deals faster",
  with a green "CLOSED WON" badge.
- **Motion:** the steps light up one after another from top to bottom (dim, then glow).
- **Lessons:** a real-looking UI fragment (the notification) inside the metaphor is a
  credibility spike. The glow is used sparingly, only on the active path.

## R6. "100 Health and Bio Angel Investors" (ECG / heartbeat, PAPER, lead magnet)
- **Hook:** heavy uppercase display. The **number in orange**, the rest in deep green.
  Header eyebrow: "8FUNDRAISING · THE ANGEL DROP · NOVEMBER 2026". Italic serif sub:
  "Doctors, founders and angel network leaders, in 19 countries."
- **Metaphor:** ECG grid paper. A heartbeat line whose **spike heights are the counts per
  country** (64 US, 8 CA, 7 UK, 2 each …), each topped by an avatar with a gold **halo**
  (angels), and flags + numbers under the baseline. The remaining small countries are
  packed into a fast flutter of small spikes labelled "11 MORE COUNTRIES · 1 EACH".
- **Stats:** 4 white cards with avatars: 38 Private angels, 32 Founders and execs, 20
  Doctors, 10 Network leaders.
- **CTA:** a full-width deep-green pill with a speech-bubble icon: COMMENT "PULSE" (the
  keyword echoes the metaphor) and "AND I SEND YOU THE FULL LIST". Channel row: LinkedIn,
  Email, "A WAY IN ON EVERY NAME".
- **Lessons:** the metaphor *is* the bar chart. A pun-level fit between the topic (health),
  the visual (ECG) and the CTA keyword (PULSE) makes it memorable.

## R7. Logo galaxy / time radar (dark, interactive)
- A centre brand mark with thousands of tiny particle dots in a fan or galaxy, rings
  labelled "Today / This week / This month", and ~30 company logo tiles with names placed
  by recency. "Faint dust farther out · Drag to explore".
- **Lessons:** scale shown as particles (thousands of dots = volume) next to named, logoed
  highlights (the ones that matter). Time as radius.

## What they all share
| Trait | Rule |
|---|---|
| Canvas | 4:5 portrait (≈1080×1350). The hero visual takes ~70%. |
| Header | Brand mark or author pill → 1–2 line headline → one-line sub. |
| Headline | 3–8 words, tight negative tracking, ONE emphasis device. |
| Density | 30–80 labelled atoms. Counts in grey. Real logos, names and numbers. |
| Grouping | Colour-coded categories or a geometric structure (grid, ring, line, funnel). |
| Type voices | Display sans · UI sans · tracked mono caps (· optional italic serif). |
| Surfaces | Cards with hairline borders and soft shadows. Logo "app tiles" with rounded squares. |
| Texture | Grid paper, dot grid, noise, colour wash, crop marks, star field. Always subtle. |
| Footer | Author headshot + name + domain, brand mark, mono proof line, or a CTA pill. |
| Motion | Build-up, routing, counting or flowing, then a hold on the full final frame. |
| Feel | A system diagram that looks like a real product UI, not a template. |
