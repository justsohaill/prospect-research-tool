# QA checklist (look at the rendered PNG, not at the code)

Fix everything in `<slug>.qa.txt` first. Then check by eye:

**Thumbnail test (view at about 1/3 size)**
- [ ] The headline reads instantly. The emphasis word pops. The metaphor is recognisable as one shape.
- [ ] There is one clear focal point, and the eye path runs headline → hero → payoff/CTA.

**Concept**
- [ ] Every major part of the metaphor carries real content (re-read the mapping table).
- [ ] It does not resemble any reference graphic's metaphor + layout, or this client's recent graphics.
- [ ] The CTA keyword (if any) fits the metaphor.

**Density & truth**
- [ ] 30–80 labelled items. No lorem ipsum, no "Tool 1", no fake-precise stats about real companies.
- [ ] Logos are real (no monogram placeholders left for important brands) and names are spelled right.
- [ ] Numbers add up (category counts sum to the total in the headline or sub).

**Craft**
- [ ] Nothing within 40 px of the edge. Crop marks and footer are aligned to the safe margin.
- [ ] Columns share x-positions. Equal items have equal spacing. Nothing is almost-aligned.
- [ ] No text overlaps a wire, icon or another label. Wires end exactly at their nodes.
- [ ] Only one emphasis device in the headline, and at most 3 font families.
- [ ] Text ≥ 11 px. Label contrast ≥ 4.5:1. Grey is used only for metadata.
- [ ] No orphan word in the headline. Line breaks are balanced.
- [ ] Texture is subtle: the grid or noise never fights the labels.
- [ ] Dark theme: the glow is only on the active or important path, and the blacks aren't muddy.

**Motion (check a mid-frame + the MP4)**
- [ ] Frame 0 already looks designed (it is the video thumbnail).
- [ ] The build follows reading order. Counters land on the exact still values.
- [ ] Final frame = PNG still. Nothing jitters, flickers or jumps at the loop.

**Identity**
- [ ] The correct client: name, domain, headshot and brand in the right slot.
- [ ] The footer proof line is true for this client.
